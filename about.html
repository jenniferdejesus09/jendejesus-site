About

Jennifer De Jesus

Healthcare operations executive and consultant. Fourteen-plus years in reproductive and community health, from the health center floor to national programs and executive leadership.

I started in 2012 as a medical assistant in a Florida health center. Everything I know about operations, I learned first from the people doing the work.

Since then I've managed a health center, built training for a Florida affiliate, co-founded a startup, and led training and organizational development at a national office, where the programs I built reached more than 8,000 employees. Most recently I was the executive partner to the leadership team of a four-site health care organization with a telehealth program, a $30M operating budget, and a team of about 90.

Today I run Jennifer De Jesus Consulting, working with reproductive and community health organizations on the operations underneath their mission. I also consider select executive roles where operations, people, and mission meet.
